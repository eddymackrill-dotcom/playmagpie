**Verdict: the site: probe is the discounted side.** Daily impressions across a full week cannot coexist with a genuinely empty index; the public `site:` operator is simply unreliable for this domain — the same class of misleading public-probe signal as Google's apex inspections (RUNBOOK site:-unreliability rule, now evidenced on Bing too). The DataForSEO `site:` probe is demoted from arbiter to secondary curiosity; the absent-from-US-top-20 check most likely reflects market mix and SERP volatility for a freshly-indexed domain, not non-serving.

**Is Bing serving MORE of the site over time? Yes, on both axes:** coverage 0 -> 39 -> 47 pages, and serving 0 -> 11 imp (single reading, 07-16) -> 100 imp / 2 clicks flowing daily (15-22 July). This is the report's most decision-relevant number and it points up. Trajectory to be tracked weekly via the owner WMT glance (impressions, clicks, non-branded query mix, coverage count).

Consequence: **IndexNow wiring is raised from queued-nice-to-have to the next infrastructure task.** Bing is the one engine currently distributing the site, its own WMT recommendations flag IndexNow, and faster change-propagation into Bing's index now directly feeds the only working organic channel (ChatGPT search and Copilot draw on Bing).
