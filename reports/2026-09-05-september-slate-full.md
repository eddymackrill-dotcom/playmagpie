  explanation then leads).

**Attribution under parallel shipping, stated plainly:** with two
pages and the CTR pass, per-page attribution survives because the
three query families are DISJOINT (txid; paypal/AU-restriction;
existing-ranker CTR), so each observable moves independently. What
parallel shipping genuinely costs even at this scale: the shared
dispatch and shared re-crawl timing mean a Bing-side systemic change
(a re-ranking of the whole site, a quota or crawl shift) is
indistinguishable from page-level effects in week one; and site-level
metrics (total clicks, total citations) will not decompose cleanly.
At eight pages this cost would have been severe; at two it is
manageable and named. Had eight shipped, per-cause attribution on
overlapping families would NOT have been possible; that is recorded
so the trade the owner weighed is visible in the record.
